[Baba Is You](https://hempuli.com/baba/) is a puzzle game where the rules you have to follow are present as physical objects in the game world and can be manipulated.
It's my favourite puzzle game.




# Custom levels
I created some levels with the in-built level editor. Here they are in chronological order (oldest first). I made a [video playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLpIwAVCnR4olYLXm4GjKeoE8Dsb56DKrV) of the solutions for each of these, mostly so that I don't forget how to complete them.

## Clogged Pipe series

Following this, I tried my hand at making five variations of the same concept: a clogged pipe.
