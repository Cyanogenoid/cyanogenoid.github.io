Asakura! P

Asakura! P is an obscure 2d platformer with action, puzzle, and metroidvania elements. I hold the speedrun world records in the any% category (beat the game as fast as possible) and 100% category (collect everything as fast as possible).

To plan the route for the 100% run, I wrote various tools. One of them uses the Z3 SMT solver to figure out the optimal order of each section in the second half of the run.

Music

I'm a huge fan of 凋叶棕 (Diao ye zong), a group that does vocal and instrumental arranges of Touhou music. They like to use la in lyrics to "censor" out references to dark or disturbing things such as death. I made a spreadsheet that counts every instance of these la's. MusicBee (my music player of choice) has a Lyrics Reloaded plugin, for which I made a TouhouWiki provider so that lyrics are automatically displayed.
